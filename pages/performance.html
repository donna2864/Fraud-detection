Insights Sumary:

Payment Methods:

  • All four payment methods (Debit Card, Credit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer) are used almost equally (~25% each).
  • Debit Card is the most used (25.2%), while Bank Transfer is the least (24.8%), but differences are minimal.
  • Customers show flexibility in payment method choice.
  • The business should maintain stable, secure support for all payment options without prioritization.

Customer Locations:

  • North Michael leads in transaction volume, followed by East Michael and West Christopher.
  • "Michael" locations dominate, suggesting geographic concentration.
  • Beyond the top three, transaction volumes are relatively balanced across other locations.

Product Categories:

  • Distribution among top categories (Home & Garden, Health & Beauty, Electronics, Toys & Games, Clothing) is very balanced (~20% each).
  • Home & Garden leads slightly (~20.3%); Health & Beauty is slightly lower (~19.8%).
  • Customer interests are diverse across multiple categories.

Fraudulent vs. Non-Fraudulent Transactions:

  • Fraudulent transactions make up about 5.17%; non-fraudulent transactions dominate (94.8%).
  • While fraud is relatively rare, it remains significant enough to require continuous monitoring.

Device Usage and Fraud:

  • Mobile devices experience the highest number of fraudulent transactions.
  • Desktops and tablets follow closely behind.
  • Emphasis on mobile security is critical.

Age and Transaction Behavior:

  • Most transactions come from customers aged 20-50.
  • Transaction amounts are generally low to moderate across all ages, with occasional high-value outliers.
  • Fraud rate spikes drastically among customers aged 70+ (~0.48), highlighting their vulnerability.

Time of Day and Fraud:

  • Fraudulent transactions are fairly consistent throughout the day.
  • Slight increase in fraud during early hours (0–5 AM) and late evening (7–9 PM).
  • Noticeable dip in fraud mid-morning (10–11 AM).
  • Fraud is not heavily time-dependent, requiring all-day vigilance.
